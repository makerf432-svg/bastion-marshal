HOW TO PLAY

Commander's Field Guide

⚔️ Marching Armies:
Click & drag from your blue settlement to any base to dispatch troops. Drag across multiple strongholds to coordinate a mass assault!
🏰 Stronghold Types & Garrison Cap:
🏛️ Town: Standard troop recruitment (1× rate, 1× damage taken).
⚔️ Armory: High-speed training grounds (2× recruitment rate). Seize these early!
🛡️ Fortress: Heavy citadel (takes only 0.5× damage). Defenders have 2× effective health.
🚩 Garrison Cap: Strongholds hold 200 to 500 troops depending on polygon size before natural recruitment pauses at MAX.
⌨️ Hotkeys & Controls:
• 1 / 2 / 3: Set army levy ratio (25%, 50%, 75%).
• Space: Select ALL your blue strongholds at once.
• R: Quick restart current battle.
• Esc / P: Pause battle.
